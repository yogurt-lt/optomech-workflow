# Double Gauss 50 mm F1.8: whole-unit manual focus

Seven elements in five groups sit in the blue optical barrel. The teal fixed housing also provides guidance. The gold focus ring rotates and translates through a helicoid, driving the barrel through a thrust coupling while the optics retain their spacing and orientation.

## Preassemble the optics

Front: G2, thick spacer, G1 and front retainer. Rear: G3, thick spacer, G4, thick spacer and G5; one rear retainer closes the stack.

## Insert into the fixed housing

Attach the mount envelope, insert the optical barrel from the front and engage two guide screws in its closed axial slots.

## Build the thrust coupling

Fit the rear washer, wind in the focus ring, then fit the front washer, keeper and lock screw. The ring rotates; the optical barrel only translates.

## Check the complete travel

0–270° gives 0 to −4.5 mm travel. Check guidance, engagement and stops; real distance markings require optical calibration.

Shoulders, thick spacers and front/rear retainers locate the optics. Two washers and a keeper capture the focus-ring lip and transmit axial motion in both directions.

Manual rotation → housing/ring helicoid → thrust coupling → optical barrel. Guide screws in straight axial slots prevent barrel rotation.

Custom single-start left-hand trapezoidal helicoid, 6 mm lead. The Canon EF rear interface is a purchased-part envelope. Zero degrees is not calibrated infinity; mirror clearance, backlash, tolerances and preload remain to be checked.

The saved native model contains 25 bodies: 18 mechanical parts and 7 optical references. Geometry checks cover seven focus poses and sampled assembly paths; they do not establish production tolerances.
