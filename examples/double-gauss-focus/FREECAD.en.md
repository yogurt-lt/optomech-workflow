# FreeCAD: Double Gauss manual focus

Open FreeCAD_Model.FCStd (v0.2.1 color fix). Edit FocusControl.AngleDeg from 0 to 270 degrees and recompute. The blue barrel translates, the gold ring rotates and translates, and the teal housing stays fixed.

To rebuild, execute FreeCAD.FCMacro and choose an output folder. Use Clipping View to inspect a section. Native sketches, revolutions, pockets and subtractive helices remain editable. Save from the graphical application to preserve appearance.
