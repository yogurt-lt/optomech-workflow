# Fixed projection · PF-2

User-run and reviewed in SolidWorks 2023.

The first three lenses have very little edge clearance. Each uses its own barrel shoulder and local adhesive bonds. The remaining groups use spacers and retaining rings.

## Assembly sequence

### Front subassembly

Install G5, the spacer and G4, then their retaining ring. Install L3, L2 and L1 onto their individual seats, bonding and curing each in a fixture.

### Rear assembly

Build the rear stack from the innermost group outward, then secure it with the rear retaining ring.

## Location and retention

Shoulders and assembly fixtures establish position; local adhesive holds the first three lenses. Access ports are not full-circumference potting grooves. The other groups transfer preload through spacers.

## Preload path

Each of the first three lenses reacts against its own barrel shoulder. The other stacks react through their spacers and seats.

## Geometry and scope

Only lens 3 has a revised mechanical rim. The revolved reference blanks do not replace the exact optical prescription.

Optical reference blanks are sampled revolved outlines, not production optical surfaces. User confirmation covers macro execution and concept review; it does not qualify tolerances, adhesive behavior, preload or stress. Original source files remain available for comparison.
