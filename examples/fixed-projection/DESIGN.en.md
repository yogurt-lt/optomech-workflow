# Fixed projection · PF-2

User-run and reviewed in SolidWorks 2023 / FreeCAD.

Center and bond each of the first three lenses in its own short cell on the bench, then install the cells in one common barrel. The large v0.3 glass flanges are removed.

## Assembly sequence

### Bench preassembly

Seat, optically center, locally bond and cure L1, L2 and L3 in their own cells; recheck before assembly. L3 has front-open adhesive windows.

### Barrel assembly

Complete the deeper optical stack first, then install L3, L2 and L1 cells. Each cell seats against its own metal shoulder.

## Location and retention

Glass is locally bonded to its cell. Each cell locates by its outer cylinder and rear face, and locks into the barrel with an external thread. No master ring is required for L1; thread teeth are omitted.

## Preload path

Each lens to its own cell shoulder; each cell to its own barrel metal shoulder. Neighboring glass elements do not transmit the locking preload.

## Geometry and scope

Nine mechanical parts and a 1 mm stop land. L1/L2 use the earlier demo outlines; L3 keeps only its small prior rim. Service replaces a lens-cell assembly; glass-only replacement requires debonding.

Optical reference blanks are sampled revolved outlines, not production optical surfaces. User confirmation covers macro execution and concept review; it does not qualify tolerances, adhesive behavior, preload or stress. Original source files remain available for comparison.
