# Microscope objective · 63X

User-run and reviewed in SolidWorks 2023.

Avoid aligning and bonding several close groups inside a deep bore. Assemble and cure each group in a short cell on the bench, then load all five cells into the common barrel.

## Assembly sequence

### Bench assembly

Insert each optical group from the −Z opening of its cell, seat and center it in a fixture, then apply and cure local adhesive bonds.

### Load complete cells

From the +Z specimen-side opening, insert the G1, G2, G3, G4 and G5 cells in order, followed by the main retaining ring.

## Location and retention

Common Ø18 cell exteriors establish barrel centering. Individual shoulders and bonds hold the glass. The main ring compresses metal cell end faces rather than the glass stack.

## Preload path

Nose retaining ring → G5 cell → G4 cell → G3 cell → G2 cell → G1 cell → barrel end shoulder.

## Geometry and scope

−Z faces the tube lens; +Z faces the specimen. STOP lies on a curved G3 surface, so no separate flat metal stop is added. Original glass outlines are retained.

Optical reference blanks are sampled revolved outlines, not production optical surfaces. User confirmation covers macro execution and concept review; it does not qualify tolerances, adhesive behavior, preload or stress. Original source files remain available for comparison.
