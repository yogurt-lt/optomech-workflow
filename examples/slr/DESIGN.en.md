# SLR lens · three barrels v5

User-run and reviewed in SolidWorks 2023 / FreeCAD.

Three structural barrels enclose the optical components. Front and rear stacks use substantial spacers. Two preassembled cells remain in the middle barrel, with one thin G8 cell in the rear.

## Assembly sequence

### Front barrel: object-side loading

G2 → tapered spacer → G1 → front retaining ring.

### Middle barrel: load complete cells

Bench-assemble the G3/G4 and G5/G6 cells. From the same front opening, insert G5/G6 first, G3/G4 second, then the common retaining ring.

### Rear barrel: image-side loading

G7 → long spacer → G8 cell → short spacer → G9 → cup spacer → G10 → rear retaining ring.

## Location and retention

G3/G4, G5/G6 and G8 are locally bonded within their cells. Other groups use shoulders, spacers and rings. The stop is integral with the teal middle barrel and has a finite aperture land.

## Preload path

Rear ring → G10 → spacer → G9 → spacer → G8 metal cell → spacer → G7 → rear-barrel shoulder. G8 glass does not carry the full stack preload.

## Geometry and scope

Version 5 removes thin bridges at the joints. Minimum uncut meridional wall distances: front 8.98, middle 8.10, rear about 8.47 mm. These are geometric checks, not stress qualification.

Optical reference blanks are sampled revolved outlines, not production optical surfaces. User confirmation covers macro execution and concept review; it does not qualify tolerances, adhesive behavior, preload or stress. Original source files remain available for comparison.
