# Wide-angle lens · two barrels

User-run and reviewed in SolidWorks 2023.

G1–G3 load from the object side of the front barrel. G4–G7 load from opposite ends of the rear barrel around its integral stop. No additional optical cells or local lens bonding are needed in this demo.

## Assembly sequence

### Front barrel: object-side loading

G3 → spacer → G2 → G1 → front ring. G1 and G2 directly contact at the new peripheral bearing band.

### Rear barrel: load from both ends

Object side: G5 → spacer → G4 → middle ring. Image side: G6 → spacer → G7 → rear ring.

### Join the barrels last

Finish both subassemblies and secure their rings before mating the 10 mm pilot joint. The rear barrel's front ring must be accessed before joining.

## Location and retention

Rectangular spacers are approximately 2.37 / 3.27 / 2.34 mm long. The integral stop has a 1 mm aperture land and an image-side 45° relief bevel.

## Preload path

Front ring → G1 → G2 → spacer → G3 → front-barrel shoulder. The two rear-barrel rings independently clamp their stacks against integral seats.

## Geometry and scope

G2/G3/G4/G7 have extended mechanical rims; the original clear-aperture sag is preserved. Both uncut barrel bodies have at least 4 mm minimum meridional wall distance.

Optical reference blanks are sampled revolved outlines, not production optical surfaces. User confirmation covers macro execution and concept review; it does not qualify tolerances, adhesive behavior, preload or stress. Original source files remain available for comparison.
