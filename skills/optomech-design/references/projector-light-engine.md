# Projector light-engine assembly

## Housing and service

- Decide the light path, optical keep-outs, opening direction and service split before drawing brackets. Consider a lower housing with integrated slide-in slots, lateral locating faces and depth stops. Let the upper housing close the escape direction and light leaks. Clear the optical surfaces and leave room for fingers or tools. Capture packages by suitable mechanical datum faces. Illumination sources can often have looser lateral clearance than relay lenses, prisms or the imager.
- For a deliberately split crossed dichroic assembly, distinguish the bonded crossover from intersections with adjacent optics. Review the real split geometry and glue access. Keep adhesive on the accessible non-optical edge and verify the locator/preload path with and without the lid as applicable.
- Locate a serviceable projection cartridge to the illumination housing with a pilot and axial mounting face. For whole-unit focusing, consider a short rotating cam ring and straight guide. State which pieces rotate, translate and remain fixed. Check travel with the lid, prism and imager in place, then verify the order for attaching and removing the followers and cartridge.

## Glass edges and turned parts

- Define the turned barrel and loading direction first. Prefer a few straight locating bores, shoulders, and rings with a single cylindrical OD. Use a straight bore or simple taper and a finite end land as needed. Avoid tiny lips and do not copy lens sag into metal spacer ends, retainer faces or barrel seats for a routine turned stack. Check available bearing width, radial wall thickness and axial height separately.
- Use an existing peripheral flat when it gives adequate bearing. If a revised glass blank is permitted, keep extensions within the individual element's edge envelope. Continue an exposed convex face at its original curvature as the first option. A concave-side flat or a ground annular land can establish a mechanical seat when the material, clear aperture and remaining edge thickness permit it. Keep added glass and machining away from adjoining cemented surfaces. Verify the original element boundaries and shared interfaces in CAD.
- Verify each group in both axial directions and in the radial pilot. Measure the actual annular contact and check adjacent glass and previous stack members during insertion. A clash-free section can still hide a floating lens. Audit the final metal face types when simple turning is the goal. Keep tolerances, preload, clear aperture and nominal CAD sampling distinct.

Use color consistently in the final CAD and illustrations. If showing an exploded view, keep cemented elements together and explain actual insertion order separately from the display offsets. Generate the exploded view and focus motion from the approved assembly geometry.
